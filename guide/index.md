# Welcome to the Footnotes Guide

Footnotes helps you learn how to dance StepMania charts.
A dancing bot shows which foot goes where, right above the arrows you're about to hit.

## Learn to dance

- [Getting Started](./footnotes/): your first song, and how to read the dancing bot.
- [Loading Songs](./footnotes/loading-songs): the demo song, songs from a link, and songs from your computer.
- [Controls](./footnotes/controls): the on-screen controls and keyboard shortcuts.
- [Tips and FAQ](./footnotes/tips): how to practice, and answers to common questions.

## Edit charts

Footnotes is built on [SMEditor](https://github.com/tillvit/smeditor), an open source StepMania chart editor by tillvit.
The full editor is part of Footnotes: press **Exit practice** to use it.
The rest of this guide covers it, from creating your first simfile to its more advanced features.
