# Controls

Everything you need while practicing is on screen. The keyboard shortcuts below make it quicker.

## On screen

**Top bar**

- **Playback:** the song's speed, as a percentage. Lower it to slow the song down.
- **Clap and metronome** are on by default. The clap marks each step, and the metronome marks each beat. In practice mode, both go quiet when you pause or the song ends. When you play the song again, the cues you enabled resume. Click either control to toggle it.
- **Speedmod:** how fast the arrows scroll. **X** scales the scroll speed with the song's tempo, and **C** keeps it constant.
- **Foot highlights:** the feet button colors each arrow blue or orange for the foot that steps it. It's the same setting as the chart editor's foot highlights.
- **3D dancer** shows or hides the dancer on the practice pad. It starts on, with the camera directly behind the dancer and looking down at 45°. Drag over the pad to rotate and tilt. Scroll or pinch to zoom. The camera stays anchored to the pad during playback. Double-click for the starting view, or use **Fit** to see the whole dancer from the current angle. Footnotes remembers your camera angle and zoom. The caption under the pad shows how to shift your weight, turn or land. After the music and the final hold end, the dancer steps back to the center one foot at a time and turns to face the game screen.
  With the camera focused, use the arrow keys to rotate, **+** and **−** to zoom, or **Home** for the rear view.
- **Fast steps** appears in the upper-left corner of the practice area when the suggested footing exceeds its movement budget at the selected playback rate. The dancer plans where to land inside each panel, lowers quick steps, and reserves time for holds and weight transfers. Its foot-speed target is 2.5 metres per second including lift. A bracket also needs enough time to turn the shoe onto both panels. The song keeps its tempo and each step lands on its beat. An amber message beside the Fast steps control names the foot, arrow, and beat of a flagged step. Click **Fast steps** to jump to the next one.
- **Loop:** repeats a section. Click it once to mark the start at the current position, move to the end of the section and click it again, and playback repeats that part until you click it a third time. The button shows the section's start and end times.

**Bottom bar**

- **Skip to start** and **Skip to end**.
- **Play** and **Pause**.
- **Time** and **Beat:** click either one to type the position you want to jump to.
- **Songs:** opens the [Songs page](https://chihduo.github.io/footnotes/songs/), to pick another song.
- **Chart** shows the current chart's level and difficulty, such as **7 Hard**. Click it to open the chart list and switch difficulty. Click outside the window or click **Chart** again to close it.
- **Footing** lets you choose **Minimal effort**, **Natural**, **Alternating** or **Free style**. Free style allows turns on four-panel singles. See [How the bot chooses your feet](./footing).
- **Exit practice:** switches to the full SMEditor chart editor.

**Note layout**

The strip on the right edge shows the whole chart. Click or drag on it to jump to that part of the song.
You can also scroll the mouse wheel over the arrows to move through the chart while it's paused.

## Keyboard

| Action                        | Keys                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Play or pause                 | <kbd>Space</kbd>                                                                                            |
| Slower or faster              | <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>→</kbd>                                               |
| Slower or faster scrolling    | <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>↓</kbd> / <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>↑</kbd>                                               |
| Back to the start, or the end | <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd>                                                                            |
| Back or forward one measure   | <kbd>Page Up</kbd> / <kbd>Page Down</kbd>                                                                   |
| Step on an arrow              | <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>, or <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> |
| Clap on every step            | <kbd>F7</kbd>                                                                                               |
| Metronome                     | <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>F7</kbd>                                                                                |
| Volume                        | <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>↓</kbd>                                                   |
| Choose another chart          | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd> on a Mac)         |
| Play along with judgments     | <kbd>P</kbd>                                                                                                |
| Leave or enter practice mode  | <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd>                                                                               |

The playback rate goes back to 100% when you reload the page.

## Dancing on a pad

Plug in a dance pad that works like a keyboard, sending the arrow keys, and dance along.
Each step lights up its target arrow, and it doesn't change the song's position or speed.
You can also step with the arrow keys or <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> on the keyboard.

If your pad sends other keys, set them in **Preferences > Gameplay keybinds...** in the chart editor (press **Exit practice** to get there).
Pads that show up as a game controller instead of a keyboard need a key mapping tool, such as JoyToKey or AntiMicroX, to send keys.

## Playing along

Press <kbd>P</kbd> to play the chart yourself, with timing judgments, while the bot keeps dancing.
Hit the arrows with your dance pad or the keyboard, as above.
Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or <kbd>P</kbd> to stop.
