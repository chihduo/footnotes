# Tips and FAQ

## Tips for learning a chart

- **Start slow.** Set the playback rate to 50% or 75%, and speed it back up as the steps start to feel natural.
- **Watch before you dance.** Play a section once just to watch the bot's feet, then dance it.
- **Practice one section at a time.** Click the note layout on the right, or press <kbd>Page Up</kbd>, to go back and repeat a hard part.
- **Listen to the steps.** Turn on the clap (<kbd>F7</kbd>) to hear a tick on every arrow.
- **Find a comfortable scroll speed.** Lower it if the arrows rush past, and raise it if they bunch up.
- **Learn the patterns' names.** Crossovers, footswitches, jacks and brackets come up in many charts. The [parity checking](../parity#tech-notation) page explains them.

## FAQ

### The dancing bot doesn't appear

The bot works with dance single, dance double, pump single and pump double charts.
For long charts, it can take a moment to work out the footing before the bot appears.

### The bot's feet look wrong

The footing is a suggestion, worked out by the rules in [How the bot chooses your feet](./footing).
It's right for most charts, but some patterns, such as decorative mines or brackets, can confuse it.
You can correct individual steps in the chart editor, as described in [Correcting the parity checker](../parity#correcting-the-parity-checker).

### My link doesn't load

Check that the link points straight to the `.sm` or `.ssc` file, that the music file is next to it, and that the site lets other pages read the files.
Links from GitHub's **Raw** button work. See [Loading songs](./loading-songs#from-a-link).

### There's no sound

Browsers only play sound after you interact with the page, so press play once.
To change the volume, press <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>↓</kbd>, or use the volume controls in the chart editor's top bar.

### Can I use a dance pad?

Yes. A dance pad that sends the arrow keys works while you practice: each step lights up its target arrow, without changing the song's position or speed.
You don't need one, though: you can dance on the floor and follow the bot.
To get timing judgments, press <kbd>P</kbd> to play along. See [Dancing on a pad](./controls#dancing-on-a-pad) and [Playing along](./controls#playing-along).

### Is Footnotes the same as SMEditor?

Footnotes is built on [SMEditor](https://github.com/tillvit/smeditor), an open source StepMania chart editor by tillvit.
It adds the practice mode and this site. The full chart editor is still there: press **Exit practice** to use it.
