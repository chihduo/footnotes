Welcome to the Footnotes Guide ​

Footnotes helps you learn how to dance StepMania charts. A dancing bot shows which foot goes where, right above the arrows you're about to hit.

Learn to dance ​

  • Getting Started: your first song, and how to read the dancing bot.
  • Loading Songs: the demo song, songs from a link, and songs from your computer.
  • Controls: the on-screen controls and keyboard shortcuts.
  • Tips and FAQ: how to practice, and answers to common questions.

Edit charts ​

Footnotes is built on SMEditor, an open source StepMania chart editor by tillvit. The full editor is part of Footnotes: press Exit practice to use it. The rest of this guide covers it, from creating your first simfile to its more advanced features.
